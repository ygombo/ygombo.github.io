abstract: "Build with AI Jakarta 2026 is a technical conference focused on moving artificial intelligence from initial concepts into scalable, production-ready enterprise applications. Organized by Google Developer Group (GDG) Jakarta, this hybrid event gathers over 300 engineering professionals to explore advanced implementations using Google AI technologies like Gemini and Vertex AI. A central highlight of the conference is the Scaling Autonomy panel discussion, which addresses the friction points of operating AI systems under heavy production loads. The session delivers strategic insights into managing high user traffic, optimizing token and infrastructure costs, preventing behavioral drift in long-running workflows, and establishing robust system observability. By sharing honest operational challenges and real-world deployment lessons, the event equips the developer community to build highly reliable, cost-efficient, and sustainable AI platforms."
