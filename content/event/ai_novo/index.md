summary: "I led a 90-minute Share & Learn session for frontline and back-office teams at PT Novo Nordisk Indonesia. We explored how large language models work, how to choose an AI tool for a given task, and how to write prompts that give AI a clear objective, relevant context, and a useful output format. In a hands-on customer profiling exercise, we used AI to pull key details from a sample customer brief and prepare for a meeting. The session closed with a practical habit participants could apply to a recurring task: give AI a clear brief, refine the response through follow-up questions, and check the result against the original information before using it."


abstract: "Better Prompts, Smarter Work was a 90-minute interactive workshop for frontline and back-office teams at PT Novo Nordisk Indonesia. The session introduced how large language models generate responses and showed how to choose AI tools for everyday work. Through a hands-on customer profiling exercise, participants practiced turning a customer brief into a structured prompt, refining the response through follow-up questions, and checking the output against the source material before using it to prepare for a meeting."
